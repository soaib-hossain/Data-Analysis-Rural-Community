# Rural Ontario Community Wellbeing Data Analysis

A data analysis project using the **Rural Ontario Institute (ROI) Community Wellbeing Dashboard** dataset. This project also serves as a hands-on way to **learn Git and GitHub** through a real-world data analysis workflow.

---

## Project Goals

1. Analyze the wellbeing of rural Ontario communities across six dimensions
2. Practice Git version control skills (commits, branches, push/pull)

---

## Dataset

**Source:** Rural Ontario Institute – Community Wellbeing Dashboard  
**File:** `ROI_All_Data_Sources.xlsx`  
**Coverage:** Rural municipalities across Ontario  
**Primary data source:** Statistics Canada 2021 Census Profile, plus additional provincial and research datasets

---

## Analysis Categories & Key Indicators

### Demographic
- Population size, density, and change (2016–2021)
- Age distribution: children, youth, young adults, adults, seniors
- Median age and population dependency ratio

### Economic
- Individual and household income
- Low income measure
- Local employment, casual employees, work-from-home rates
- Internet connectivity (50/10 Mbps broadband access)
- Average industry wages and job changes (2021–2022)

### Environment
- Biocapacity and ecological footprint (Gha/capita)
- Proportion of protected area
- Climate change projections (growing season, temperature, precipitation)
- Commuting patterns (car use, local commute, long commute)

### Health
- Number and types of health facilities (2023)
- Long-term care spaces and wait times
- Residents aged 85+
- Mental health service calls and trends (2022–2023)

### Housing
- Core housing need, unaffordable, inadequate, and unsuitable housing rates
- One-person households
- Shelter costs for owned and rented dwellings

### Society
- Post-secondary education attainment
- Indigenous identity, immigrant, and racialized populations
- Gender diversity
- Arts & cultural facilities and charitable arts organizations
